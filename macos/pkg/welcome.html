SpliX for macOS

This installs a native, open-source printer driver for Samsung laser printers (and their Xerox, Dell and Toshiba twins) that stopped working on macOS 27 because Samsung's driver was Intel-only and Rosetta is gone.

What the installer does:

  • Installs the rastertoqpdl print filter and 82 printer descriptions (PPDs).
  • Looks for Samsung printers connected by USB or shared on the network (AirPort Express, Time Capsule) and adds them automatically.
  • Repairs printers that macOS already added with the broken Samsung driver.

Switch the printer on and connect it before continuing, so it can be detected. Nothing is sent to the printer during installation.
